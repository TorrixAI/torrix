# Code of conduct

Torrix is built in the open, and we want everyone who takes part to feel welcome: in issues,
discussions, pull requests and anywhere else the project meets people.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. People join with very different backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you judge.
- Criticize ideas and code, never the person.
- Keep discussions on topic and useful for the next person who reads them.
- Respect people's time and privacy. Do not share anyone's personal information or private
  messages without their permission.

## What is not okay

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes or language about anyone's identity or background.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Repeatedly pushing a point after you have been asked to stop.
- Spam, or using the project's spaces to promote unrelated products.

## What happens if someone breaks it

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock threads, and warn, mute or ban people, depending on
how serious and how repeated the behavior is.

## Reporting

If someone makes you feel unsafe or unwelcome, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the
comment, or contact the maintainers privately at the email address on the
[TorrixAI GitHub profile](https://github.com/TorrixAI). Reports are handled confidentially, and you
will not be treated worse for making one.
